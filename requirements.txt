We are rebuilding this project completely from scratch.

The previous project/implementation was called SwaggerGuard. Do NOT preserve the old SwaggerGuard implementation, package identity, architecture, UI, or branding. This is a clean rebuild with a new product identity:

PRODUCT NAME:
UnveiledLens

TAGLINE:
Discover Beyond the Known.

PRODUCT POSITIONING:
UnveiledLens is an ownership-verified, SerpApi-powered external exposure auditor.

Its purpose is to discover publicly indexed API/storage resources and other relevant exposure signals associated with a verified domain, safely determine whether discovered resources appear accessible without authentication, classify the exposure, and provide privacy-conscious security findings.

CORE PRODUCT LOOP:
Discover -> Verify -> Classify -> Safely Validate -> Interpret -> Inform

IMPORTANT POSITIONING:
The product starts from the perspective of:

“What has the public web already revealed about this organization?”

This makes search-engine intelligence a core discovery mechanism rather than a decorative feature.

Do NOT claim that UnveiledLens discovers every exposed API, every vulnerability, or every security issue.

Use accurate language such as:

* “Identifies publicly discoverable exposure signals through search-engine intelligence.”
* “Discovers what search engines reveal about your external surface.”
* “Turns publicly discoverable exposure signals into actionable security findings.”
